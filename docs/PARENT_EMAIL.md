# Parent Email — Digital Futures Launch

**Subject: Introducing Digital Futures — your child's new digital skills & AI course**

Dear Parents and Carers,

I am pleased to introduce **Digital Futures**, a new digital skills and AI-literacy course
that your child will begin this term as part of their Computer Science lessons.

**What the course is for**

The world your child is growing up in runs on digital tools and, increasingly, on
artificial intelligence. Digital Futures is designed to make sure every pupil becomes
genuinely confident and responsible with that technology — not just using it, but creating
with it, thinking critically about it, and staying safe online. Pupils develop real,
transferable skills: professional communication, working with data, design, online safety,
and the careful, age-appropriate use of AI. The course is built on Microsoft 365, the same
software used throughout the school.

The curriculum has been written by a **senior examiner in both Computer Science and AI**,
and is taught by our **specialist Computer Science teachers**.

**How it runs**

Years 8 and 9 have one Digital Futures lesson per fortnight. Pupils also have access to
supporting activities they can explore at home through the school's Computer Science site.

**Your child's privacy**

Protecting pupils' privacy is central to how we have set this up:

- Pupils log in with an **anonymous username** (a class code and a number) — **no names or
  email addresses are stored** in the system.
- Pupils **cannot sign themselves up**; all accounts are created and managed by staff.
- Younger pupils never interact directly with a live AI tool — any AI activities are
  guided and reviewed by their teacher.

Your child will be given a login card with their username and password in their first
lesson. There is nothing you need to set up at home.

If you have any questions about the course, please do get in touch.

Yours faithfully,

[Your name]
[Head of Computer Science]
